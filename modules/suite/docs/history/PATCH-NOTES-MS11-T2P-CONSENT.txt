Newfie's Server Suite 1.2.35 - T2P Consent

- /t2p <player> no longer teleports immediately.
- The destination player must accept with /tpaccept within 60 seconds.
- After acceptance, /t2p waits 10 seconds and then teleports the requester to the target player's current position.
- /tpaccept now handles both /t2p and /t2m consent requests.
- The requester's configured role/cooldown policy is rechecked at acceptance and completion.
- The /t2p cooldown is charged only after a successful accepted teleport.
- Disconnects, jail state, disabled commands, conflicting teleports, and expired requests cancel safely.

Unchanged companion versions:
- Client Integrity / Anti-Cheat Client 1.1.26 (protocol 13)
- Discord 1.0.8
- Community MOTD 2.1.2
