# MemoryAudit1 changelog — 1.2.43

Changes since the supplied Suite 1.2.42 build. Website source remains 2.7.3; Website Connector remains 1.1.1.

- **Server Essentials Suite 1.2.43:** PacketGuard now expires and bounds diagnostic deduplication identities, removes them on disconnect, and resets telemetry when its service stops.
- **Server Essentials Suite 1.2.43:** Mod DB verification now caps waiting/in-progress work at 512, runtime results at 1,024 entries and an estimated 64 MiB. Installed-server baselines take priority during eviction. Deferred work retries from current session/baseline state; cancellation releases pending reservations and stops late result publication.
- **Server Essentials Suite 1.2.43:** Memory diagnostics explain substantial GC fragmentation when the post-GC floor is not rising. No automatic forced GC or world-unload behavior was added.
- **Discord Integration 1.0.12:** Expired link codes, issuance cooldown records, and failed-attempt histories are pruned, including during idle periods. Pending codes and tracked identities have limits; shutdown clears temporary linking state. Active cooldowns and rate limits remain enforced.
- **Community MOTD 2.1.5:** Tab changes/live refreshes dispose old GUI composers and textures. Repeated MOTD requests reuse one dialog. Closing releases textures; shutdown unregisters the dialog and removes its temporary logo file.
- **Client Integrity 1.1.28:** Transfer consent dialogs release textures and unregister after closing. Accept/decline remains a one-time decision. Protocol 13 and networkVersion 1.1.21 are unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** ChatGuard 2.1.3, Player List 1.1.1, Website Connector 1.1.1, website 2.7.3.

These are confirmed source-level retention/resource defects and bounds improvements. They do not prove that this mod family accounts for the reported 16.7 GB process memory. See AUDIT-1.2.43-MEMORY.md for scope and remaining investigation.
