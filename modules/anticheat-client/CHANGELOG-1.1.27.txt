Newfies Client Integrity 1.1.27

OreShield live-server performance
- OreShield sync protocol v3.
- Treats a target chunk unloading before apply as a harmless skip instead of a failed apply.
- Removes duplicate client-initiated resync recovery; genuine apply failures are reported once to the server for bounded recovery.
- Removes the redundant immediate/deferred receipt ACK so normal OreShield traffic uses one final ACK per sequence.
- Extends the OreShield readiness hello interval from 5 seconds to 30 seconds.
- Preserves the existing reveal/remesh, mining cancellation and sequence-ordering behavior.

Anti-Cheat protocol remains 13.
